ingredientsImageAlt: Flour, yeast, milk, yogurt, sugar, salt, baking powder, vegetable oil, and butter on a table
ingredients:
  - items:
      - item: high-grade wheat flour
        amount: 250 g
      - item: instant dry yeast
        amount: 3–4 g
        note: half of a standard 7 g packet
      - item: sugar
        amount: 5 g (1 tsp)
      - item: salt
        amount: ¾ tsp
      - item: baking powder
        amount: ½ tsp
      - item: warm milk
        amount: 100 ml
      - item: plain natural yogurt
        amount: 75 g
      - item: vegetable oil
        amount: 1 tbsp
      - item: ghee or butter, for brushing
        amount: 20 g
steps:
  - title: Activate the yeast
    text: Combine the warm milk (35–40 °C), yeast, and sugar. Leave for 10 minutes, until foamy.
    image: ./images/step-1.webp
    imageAlt: Activated yeast mixture foaming in a glass
  - title: Knead the dough
    text: Mix the flour, salt, and baking powder in a bowl. Add the yeast mixture, yogurt, and vegetable oil. Knead for 8–10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and elastic. It should remain soft and slightly sticky.
    image: ./images/step-2.webp
    imageAlt: Soft dough being kneaded by hand in a bowl
  - title: Let the dough rise
    text: Cover the bowl with plastic wrap or a towel and leave in a warm place for 1 hour, until the dough has doubled in size.
    image: ./images/step-3.webp
    imageAlt: Risen dough in a glass bowl covered with a towel
  - title: Divide the dough
    text: Punch down the dough, divide it into 4 equal pieces, and shape them into balls. Cover with a towel and rest for 15 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-4.webp
    imageAlt: Four dough balls on a floured surface
  - title: Roll out the flatbreads
    text: Roll each ball into an oval or teardrop about 5 mm thick. Lightly spritz one side with water.
    image: ./images/step-5.webp
    imageAlt: A rolled flatbread and dough balls beside a rolling pin
  - title: Cook the naan
    text: Heat a dry skillet (cast iron is best) over high heat. Place a flatbread in the pan, wet side down, and cover with a lid. When bubbles appear (1–2 minutes), flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes, until golden spots form.
    image: ./images/step-6.webp
    imageAlt: Naan flatbread cooking in a dry cast-iron skillet
  - title: Brush with butter and serve
    text: Brush the hot flatbreads with melted butter right away. Serve warm, covered with a towel.
    image: ./images/step-7.webp
    imageAlt: A hot naan flatbread being brushed with melted butter
tips:
  - For garlic naan, add 1–2 minced garlic cloves and a little chopped cilantro to the melted butter.
  - Made with water instead of milk, the flatbreads brown a little less.
