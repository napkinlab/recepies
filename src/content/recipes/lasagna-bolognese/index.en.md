ingredients:
  - title: For the ragù
    items:
      - item: olive oil
        amount: 2 tbsp
      - item: onion
        amount: 1 medium onion
      - item: carrot
        amount: 1 medium
      - item: celery
        amount: 1 stalk
      - item: garlic
        amount: 2 cloves
      - item: ground beef
        amount: 400 g
      - item: ground pork
        amount: 200 g
      - item: dry red wine
        amount: 100 ml
      - item: tomato paste
        amount: 2 tbsp
      - item: tomatoes in their own juice
        amount: 400 g
      - item: salt
        amount: 1 tsp
      - item: black pepper
        amount: ½ tsp
  - title: For the béchamel
    items:
      - item: butter
        amount: 50 g
      - item: wheat flour
        amount: 50 g
      - item: milk
        amount: 700 ml
      - item: salt
        amount: ½ tsp
      - item: ground nutmeg
        amount: a pinch
  - title: For assembly
    items:
      - item: lasagna sheets
        amount: 250 g
      - item: grated Parmesan
        amount: 120 g
steps:
  - title: Make the soffritto
    text: Finely chop the onion and celery and grate the carrot. Heat the oil in a deep skillet and sauté the vegetables for about 10 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute.
    image: ./images/step-1.png
    imageAlt: Chopped vegetables for soffritto sautéing in a deep skillet
  - title: Brown the meat
    text: Add both kinds of ground meat, breaking up any lumps, and fry over high heat for about 8 minutes, until browned.
    image: ./images/step-2.png
    imageAlt: Ground beef and pork browning with the vegetables
  - title: Add the wine and tomatoes
    text: Pour in the wine and reduce it almost completely. Add the tomato paste, tomatoes, salt, and pepper.
    image: ./images/step-3.png
    imageAlt: Meat ragù with red wine, tomato paste, and tomatoes
  - title: Simmer the ragù
    text: Reduce the heat to low and simmer the ragù covered for about 40 minutes, stirring occasionally. The sauce should become thick.
    image: ./images/step-4.png
    imageAlt: Thick meat ragù simmering covered
  - title: Make the béchamel
    text: Melt the butter, add the flour, and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually pour in the warm milk, stirring constantly. Add the salt and nutmeg, and cook for about 10 minutes, until thickened.
    image: ./images/step-5.png
    imageAlt: Béchamel sauce thickening in a saucepan as it is stirred
  - title: Assemble the lasagna
    text: Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Spread a thin layer of béchamel in the dish, then add sheets, ragù, béchamel, and Parmesan. Repeat the layers 3–4 times. Finish with béchamel and plenty of Parmesan.
    image: ./images/step-6.png
    imageAlt: Lasagna layers with sheets, ragù, béchamel, and Parmesan in a baking dish
  - title: Bake the lasagna
    text: Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until golden brown.
    image: ./images/step-7.png
    imageAlt: Lasagna baking under foil in the oven
  - title: Let the lasagna rest
    text: Let the lasagna stand for 10–15 minutes so the layers can set and it will not fall apart when sliced.
    image: ./images/step-8.png
    imageAlt: Finished Bolognese lasagna with a golden crust before slicing
tips:
  - If the sheets do not require pre-cooking, use them dry, but make sure the ragù and béchamel are thin enough.
  - Lasagna is especially delicious the next day.
