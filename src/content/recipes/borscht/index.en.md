ingredients:
  - items:
      - item: bone-in beef
        amount: 600 g
      - item: water
        amount: 2.5–3 L
      - item: beetroot
        amount: 300 g
      - item: lemon juice or vinegar
        amount: 1 tbsp
      - item: sunflower oil
        amount: 2–3 tbsp
      - item: medium onion
        amount: "1"
      - item: large carrot
        amount: "1"
      - item: tomato paste
        amount: 2 tbsp
      - item: potatoes
        amount: 400 g
      - item: white cabbage
        amount: 300 g
      - item: garlic
        amount: 3 cloves
      - item: bay leaves
        amount: "2"
      - item: salt
        amount: about 2 tsp
        note: to taste
      - item: sugar
        amount: 1 tsp
      - item: ground black pepper
        amount: ½ tsp
      - item: fresh herbs
        amount: 30 g
        note: dill and parsley
steps:
  - title: Make the broth
    text: Rinse the meat, cover it with cold water, and bring to a boil. Skim off the foam, reduce the heat to low, and simmer covered for 1½–2 hours, until the meat comes easily away from the bone. Add about 1 tsp salt halfway through cooking.
    image: ./images/step-1.jpg
    imageAlt: Bone-in beef simmering in a pot with a bay leaf
  - title: Prepare the beef
    text: Remove the meat from the broth and let it cool slightly. Separate it from the bone, cut it into pieces, and return it to the pot. Discard the bone.
    image: ./images/step-2.jpg
    imageAlt: Cooked beef cut into pieces on a cutting board
  - title: Simmer the beetroot
    text: Coarsely grate the beetroot and place it in a pan with a few spoonfuls of broth and the lemon juice or vinegar. Cover and simmer for about 10 minutes; the acid helps preserve its bright color.
    image: ./images/step-3.jpg
    imageAlt: Grated beetroot simmering in a pan
  - title: Make the sautéed vegetables
    text: Fry the chopped onion and grated carrot in the sunflower oil. Add the tomato paste and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
    image: ./images/step-4.jpg
    imageAlt: Fried onion and carrot with tomato paste in a pan
  - title: Add the potatoes
    text: Cut the potatoes into cubes, add them to the boiling broth, and cook for about 10 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-5.jpg
    imageAlt: Diced potatoes cooking in beef broth
  - title: Add the cabbage
    text: Shred the cabbage, add it to the pot, and cook for another 10 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-6.jpg
    imageAlt: Shredded cabbage cooking in broth with potatoes
  - title: Add the sautéed vegetables and beetroot
    text: Add the sautéed vegetables and beetroot to the pot. Add the crushed garlic, bay leaves, remaining salt, sugar, and pepper. Cook for 5–7 minutes, taste, and adjust the salt, sugar, or lemon juice or vinegar if needed.
    image: ./images/step-7.jpg
    imageAlt: Beef and vegetables simmering in borscht with bay leaves
  - title: Let the borscht rest
    text: Remove the pot from the heat, add the finely chopped herbs, cover, and leave to rest for 30 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-8.jpg
    imageAlt: Finished beef borscht with herbs and sour cream
tips:
  - Serve hot with sour cream and rye bread rubbed with garlic.
  - For a clear broth, do not let it boil vigorously and skim off the foam at the beginning.
  - If you like thicker borscht, add a can of red kidney beans at the end.
  - Borscht tastes even better the next day.
